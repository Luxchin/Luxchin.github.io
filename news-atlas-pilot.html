A pilot tool for exploring climate technologies

I have published the first pilot release of the Climate Technology Atlas, an interactive tool that organises 458 mitigation and adaptation technology records from the CTCN taxonomy.

The pilot goes beyond a list of equipment. It brings together hardware, software and orgware, together with infrastructure, energy, materials, supplies and an exploratory view of raw-material supply geography.

The tool is under methodological and technical review. Its classifications and analytical descriptions will continue to be refined through sector-specific validation.
