Three questions before exploring

What is this atlas?

This interactive atlas organises and expands 458 mitigation and adaptation technology records using the taxonomy of the Climate Technology Centre and Network (CTCN). The CTCN connects countries with climate technology solutions and, at the request of developing countries, advances their development and transfer through technical assistance, capacity building, specialised information and a worldwide network of companies and institutions. The atlas turns that taxonomy into a visual pathway and examines every technology as an integrated system of Hardware, Software and Orgware, together with its implementation, life-cycle and supply conditions. About the CTCN ↗ · Main website ↗

What can I use it for?

The atlas is a reference and early-stage decision-support tool. It organises information so that you can move from a climate need to a sector, a group and a specific technology. Each profile brings together technical function, applicability, nature, form, environmental function, infrastructure and energy requirements, inputs, Hardware, Software and Orgware, critical raw materials, countries relevant to extraction and processing, and supporting sources. This integrated view helps frame questions, identify capabilities and dependencies, recognise information gaps and prepare studies, road maps, terms of reference or technical discussions on a clearer basis.

How did it originate?

The atlas responds to the need to turn technology information produced through United Nations climate processes into a visual experience, as that information often remains scattered across lengthy and difficult documents. Its construction combines several analytical layers: the CTCN taxonomy provides the hierarchy of mitigation, adaptation, sectors, groups and technologies; the IIASA concept structures Hardware, Software and Orgware; sector-specific technical sources support descriptions and rationales; technology-type references inform characterisation; and USGS, IEA and World Bank sources guide the exploratory analysis of raw materials and production and processing geographies. These sources are harmonised across three language datasets and a shared structure for search, traceability and technology reading.

01 / Start

Are you looking for a technology to mitigate emissions or adapt to climate impacts?

First step · Define your starting pointOpen guidance

If you already know the technology or problem you want to investigate, enter a keyword — for example, lithium, sensors, water or energy. For a broader search, begin with Mitigation to explore solutions that reduce or avoid emissions, or Adaptation to find technologies that respond to climate risks and impacts. You can change or clear your selection at any time.

02 / Taxonomy

Sector distribution of the active scope

Each rectangle represents a sector. Its area shows that sector’s share of the active branch and search scope.

Denominator: 458 records
Second step · Locate the application sectorOpen guidance

Notice how the map reorganises sectors around your search or climate purpose. Each rectangle’s size shows how many records that sector contributes to the active result. Select the sector most closely related to your need to continue to its technology groups; select it again to remove that filter.

The treemap sectors add up to 100% of the active scope. Click a sector to filter; click it again to remove the filter.

03 / Technology group

Technology groups in the selected sector

Select one group to reveal its technologies.

Third step · Refine the search by groupOpen guidance

Each group brings together technologies with a related purpose or operating principle. Read the group names and available record counts, then select the one that most closely matches your need. To explore another route, return to the previous sector without starting over.

04 / Explorer

Technologies in the selected group

Select a technology to open its complete profile.

Fourth step · Select a technologyOpen guidance

Review the technologies in the group and open the one that best matches your interest. Its profile brings together a technical explanation, characterisation attributes, implementation conditions, components, raw materials, supply geography and sources. Open another card to consider the information in sequence.
